# 🚀 SamNPlayer — Free by Design. Built for Better Playback.
Hey everyone! 👋

We're introducing **SamNPlayer**, a desktop application for synchronized video playback, multi-axis Funscript control and script creation. We built it with a simple goal: useful tools, reliable playback and **free access by design**.

**No subscription. No license-key activation.** SamNPlayer is proprietary freeware, and we're looking for people who want to help test it.

## What can it do?
- **Multi-axis playback:** synchronize video and supported Funscript axes.
- **Audio-driven vibration (new):** optionally derive vibration from video audio. It's off by default, falls back to script vibration when audio analysis is unavailable, and leaves suction script-controlled.
- **BPM “From audio” (new):** estimate tempo from up to the first ten minutes of audio. It's an informational aid; it does not rewrite your scripts.
- **“Write into script” (new):** apply a playback offset to script data, including axes, bookmarks and chapters.
- **Creation tools:** work with script generation and playback in the same desktop application.
